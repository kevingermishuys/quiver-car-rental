Rates run on 24 hours, not calendar days

A rental day starts the moment you take the vehicle and runs for a full 24 hours. Collect at 16:00 and your first day ends at 16:00 the next day — so an afternoon pick-up costs you nothing in lost time, which matters when you are arriving on an international flight.

Collection & delivery

Fixed Collection Points

Collection and delivery

We are based in Swakopmund, on the Namibian coast — most rentals are collected and returned here. For your security, we also hand over at two fixed points: Hosea Kutako International Airport (Windhoek) and Walvis Bay International Airport. We do not deliver to private addresses, hotels or any other location in Namibia.

Handover at Hosea Kutako or Walvis Bay Airport carries a fixed transfer fee, confirmed in your quote up front — nothing to discover at handover.

How to Book

How to book

01

Send us your dates

Message us on WhatsApp or email with your dates, how long you need the vehicle, and where you'd like to collect it — Swakopmund, Hosea Kutako or Walvis Bay Airport.

02

We confirm and quote

We check the vehicle is free for your dates and come back with a quote — including the transfer fee if you are meeting us at Hosea Kutako or Walvis Bay Airport.

03

Collect or meet us

Pick the vehicle up from us in Swakopmund, or meet us at Hosea Kutako or Walvis Bay International Airport on the day.

Booking from outside Namibia?

That is most of who we deal with. Everything happens in English over WhatsApp or email — there is no account to create and no booking platform to fight with. We are on Central Africa Time (UTC+2), so allow for the time difference when you are waiting on a reply.
