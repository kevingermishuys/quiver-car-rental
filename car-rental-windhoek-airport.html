Car rental at Windhoek Airport (Hosea Kutako)

Collect a Toyota Fortuner 4x4 at Hosea Kutako International Airport (WDH): unlimited kilometres and rental days counted in full 24-hour periods from collection.

Collect your Fortuner at Hosea Kutako International Airport (Windhoek)

Hosea Kutako International Airport (WDH) is where most visitors arrive in Namibia. Instead of arranging a transfer into town, you can meet us at the international terminal and drive away in a Toyota Fortuner 4x4 – a full-size 7-seat 4x4 with an automatic gearbox and room for luggage. We rent only this one vehicle, so you know exactly what you are getting.

Driving on from Windhoek

Hosea Kutako is a good starting point for a self-drive trip across Namibia. Our base in Swakopmund is roughly 360 km west of Windhoek, about four hours by road on tarred highway. The Fortuner handles tarred highways, maintained gravel and district roads, and the salt roads around the coast.

Because rental days run for 24 hours from collection, an afternoon arrival costs you nothing in lost time. Read more about places our clients have driven.

What you need to rent

  • A valid ID or passport and a valid driver's licence (you can upload photos when you book).
  • The renter must be at least 23 years old and have held a licence for at least 2 years.
  • Only the renter, or an additional driver agreed with us in writing before collection, may drive.

Frequently asked questions

Can I collect the vehicle at Windhoek airport?

Yes. We hand over the Toyota Fortuner 4x4 at Hosea Kutako International Airport (WDH), the international airport serving Windhoek. Choose Hosea Kutako as your pickup location when you book.

Do you deliver to hotels or addresses in Windhoek?

No. For your security we hand over at three fixed points only: Swakopmund, Hosea Kutako International Airport and Walvis Bay International Airport. We do not deliver to private addresses or hotels.

How much does it cost to collect at Hosea Kutako?

The day rate for Hosea Kutako pickup is N$1,900 per 24-hour period, with unlimited kilometres and a minimum of three days. The airport handover is included in that rate, and the refundable deposit is N$3,500.

Can I return the vehicle somewhere else?

You choose a pickup and a return location when you book. Handover and return take place in Swakopmund, at Hosea Kutako or at Walvis Bay International Airport. Message us on WhatsApp if you want to plan a route that ends somewhere different.

I land in the afternoon. Do I lose a rental day?

No. A rental day is a full 24 hours from the moment you take the vehicle, so collecting at 16:00 means your first day ends at 16:00 the next day.
